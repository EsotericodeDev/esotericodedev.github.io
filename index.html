How We Work

A simple engagement

  1. Scope

    We agree on goals, assets in scope, rules of engagement, and data handling.

  2. Test

    Manual testing, supported by tooling, focused on the issues that matter to your business.

  3. Report

    Prioritized findings with clear reproduction steps, remediation guidance, and a readout with your team.

  4. Retest

    We confirm your fixes work, so you have a clean record for customers and auditors.
